# The virtual combat log: what Forever took away, how to detect it from the observation log, how to simulate it

Date: 2026-10-14 · Client: WoW Forever 1.60.1.70338 (interface 16001) · Branch `research/virtual-cleu` · Research only, no code.

**Updated with probe session O** (Warrior, level 21, 2026-10-13/14; `docs/research/2026-10-14-probe-session-O-results.md`). `UNIT_COMBAT` came back plain, so the sections below record both the pre-probe reasoning and the measured result, marked **[MEAS O-On]**. 21 rows changed status.

## 0. Summary (ten lines)

1. Addons get no combat log on Forever. Registering `COMBAT_LOG_EVENT_UNFILTERED` raises the "Blizzard UI only" popup, and the reader exists only in the secure namespace. So all 54 retail subevents and every damage, heal and miss field are gone as CLEU events (session O recovered many of them through other, plain events: see lines 2 and 5).
2. Most of the stream can be given back as derived events. We have the player's own plain cast events, `PLAYER_SWING` (plain, per hand), plain usability and cooldown flags, plain combo points, plain target facts (including death and threat), plain loss-of-control data, plain weapon-enchant reads and plain UI errors. **Session O added the biggest one: `UNIT_COMBAT` is plain for every unit token, in combat (event type, flagText, amount, school), plus plain `UNIT_DIED`, `PARTY_KILL` and `PLAYER_TARGET_DIED` [MEAS O-O1, O6].**
3. Each derived event carries a provenance: observed, inferred (from those signals) or modelled (from DB2 spell data).
4. What we still cannot give back: overkill, absorbed amounts and over-energize; the **source** of any hit (`UNIT_COMBAT` is victim-side, so in a group hits on one unit mix, and third-party hits on units we hold no token for are gone); IMMUNE, RESIST and ABSORB outcomes are plain-channel but were not seen yet. We also lose the identity of other units' casts and auras (spellID, castGUID and `interruptedBy` are secret; the event and `castBarID` are plain), dispels, summons and other third-party events. Early removal of our debuffs has only a timing mark (`UNIT_AURA` `removedAuraInstanceIDs` present in combat), not which aura [MEAS O-O4, O12].
5. **Crits are detected directly now**: `UNIT_COMBAT` flagText CRITICAL (also GLANCING, CRUSHING, BLOCK, BLOCK_REDUCED) arrives plain for the target, nameplate units and the player [MEAS O-O1: 779 firings, 0 secret, 752 in combat]. The class proxies (combo-point jump of 2, Flurry shortening the next `PLAYER_SWING`, Clearcasting-style cost drop) remain only for hits on units without a token.
6. Inventory: 137 rows (70 CLEU subevent rows including perspective splits, 10 miss-type rows, 57 non-CLEU event rows).
7. Of the 137 rows, **59 are reconstructable, 52 partial, 19 impossible** and 7 not applicable to the 1.6 ruleset (was 42 / 65 / 23 / 7 before session O; 21 rows changed: 17 up to reconstructable, 4 impossible rows up to partial). The sixteen rows predicted to improve with O1 or O2 mostly did; M7, M9 (and M5) only reach partial until IMMUNE / RESIST are seen, and C26, C51, C52, C60 stay partial.
8. **Recipe 1 (D1 + D2 + D3):** the cast-driven aura ledger. `UNIT_SPELLCAST_*` (player) plus DB2 durations give SPELL_CAST_*, SPELL_AURA_APPLIED/REFRESH/REMOVED for own buffs and target debuffs, and the periodic tick grid (D4).
9. **Recipe 2 (D7 + O1):** the swing ledger. `PLAYER_SWING` gives SWING_* timing and hand; the target's `UNIT_COMBAT` WOUND / MISS / DODGE / PARRY gives the outcome, amount and crit (observed, victim-side). The same-frame `UNIT_POWER_FREQUENT` burst and the Overpower flip (D8) are now cross-checks, with known leaks [MEAS O-O3].
10. **Recipe 3 (D9 + D11 + D15):** flips and facts. Unexplained usability, overlay and cost flips give procs (the overlay event never fired for a Warrior). `UNIT_DIED`, `PARTY_KILL` and `PLAYER_TARGET_DIED` give deaths directly. Combo-point deltas remain a hit cross-check. **Open after session O:** amounts unchecked against ground truth, periodic ticks, the source of a hit in groups, same-frame ordering against swings and casts, IMMUNE / RESIST / ABSORB, other classes (§5, results doc §22).

## 1. Scope, method, evidence keys

**Scope.** The inventory covers:
- every `COMBAT_LOG_EVENT_UNFILTERED` subevent in the retail list: the 54 Blizzard's own combat-log filter declares (`FUI Blizzard_CombatLog/Mainline/Blizzard_CombatLog.lua:40-125`), plus `SPELL_ABSORBED` and `SPELL_HEAL_ABSORBED`, which retail emits and Blizzard's filter list omits (Hekili's Brewmaster module consumes the first, `Hk TheWarWithin/MonkBrewmaster.lua:908, 947`);
- every miss type;
- every non-CLEU combat event whose payload turned secret, that stopped firing, that is new on Forever, or that we use as a substitute.

Where the verdict depends on who the source or target is, the row is split by perspective. The perspectives are: player → target (ours), enemy → player (on us), and others.

**Verdicts** (column V):
- **R**: reconstructable. Observed directly, or inferred or modelled with high confidence for the perspective named.
- **P**: partial. Timing without amount, some cases only, or class-specific.
- **I**: impossible with today's measured signals.
- **n/a**: no such mechanic in the 1.6 ruleset.

The verdict is for today's measured signals. "→ R if O1" marks an upgrade a probe could unlock.

**Provenance** follows the brief:
- *observed*: a plain client signal says it happened;
- *inferred*: plain signals plus a rule imply it;
- *modelled*: spell data and a clock predict it, with no confirming signal.

Confidence is high, medium or low, with the failure modes stated in §3.

**Priority** (column Pri):
- **P1**: feeds a rotation root or the belief today.
- **P2**: pack trackers or display.
- **P3**: other addon types only.
- **P4**: no known consumer.

**Method notes.**
- The `wow` MCP server's event index was diffed between the flavours (`forever` vs `retail`).
- The MCP server's Forever *UI-source* index was not synced, so `wow_ui_grep` refused flavour `forever`. The generated docs and UI source were read directly from the local cache (build 1.60.1.70338; R02 used the same copy).
- Name diff of the documented events, Forever against live: Forever adds `PLAYER_SWING`, `PLAYER_SWING_RANGE_UPDATE`, `UNIT_HAPPINESS`, `UNIT_PET_TRAINING_POINTS` and `PET_STATS_UPDATE`. Every other combat-, damage-, loss-, absorb- and attack-named event is identical in both.
- CLEU payload shapes are not in the docs: `COMBAT_LOG_EVENT_UNFILTERED` has no `Payload`, and `C_CombatLogSecure.GetCurrentEventInfo` returns nothing documented. The field lists below are the public retail shapes [ASSUMED].
- Shared base fields, not repeated per row: timestamp, subevent, hideCaster, sourceGUID/Name/Flags/RaidFlags, destGUID/Name/Flags/RaidFlags. `SPELL_*`, `RANGE_*` and `SPELL_PERIODIC_*` add spellId, spellName and spellSchool.

**The one fact under every row.** The combat log is forbidden to addons on Forever:
- Registering it raises Blizzard's "blocked from an action only available to the Blizzard UI" popup [MEAS GS; ForeverProbe `Legacy.lua:461-467`].
- Both log events are `HasRestrictions` [DOC CL:122-136].
- The data getter lives in `C_CombatLogSecure` with `Environment = "SecureOnly"` [DOC CLS:6].
- `CombatLogGetCurrentEventInfo` is only a deprecation alias, loaded behind a CVar, to a function absent from `C_CombatLog` [FUI Blizzard_DeprecatedCombatLog/Deprecated_CombatLog.lua:4-18].
- Blizzard's own log processor reads through the secure getter [FUI Blizzard_CombatLogProcessor/Blizzard_CombatLogProcessor.lua:105].
- The chat log still shows lines. They reach Blizzard's frame through `COMBAT_LOG_MESSAGE` as a `|K`-protected string [DOC CLS:126-136; FUI Blizzard_CombatLog/Mainline/Blizzard_CombatLog.lua:1678-1686].

So in every CLEU row below, "gone" means this fact; it is not cited again.

## 4. Simulation notes (forecast side)

The forecast emits derived events with provenance *modelled*, plus a confidence equal to 1 for deterministic events and to the proc curve's effectiveness for proc events. The near-horizon rule (job brief `p3-lanes.md`, user rules of 2026-10-13 23:45 and 2026-10-14 00:00):
- nothing probabilistic inside the close horizon (default 3 s);
- procs ramp convexly beyond it, up to the pack's maximum at the simulation's end;
- additive effects only in Phase 3;
- crits are never assumed at any horizon.

| Derived event (modelled) | Emitted by the sim? | Inside the close horizon | Beyond it |
|---|---|---|---|
| SPELL_CAST_START / SUCCESS (our simulated actions) | yes, every simulated action | yes (deterministic) | yes |
| SPELL_AURA_APPLIED / REFRESH / APPLIED_DOSE / REMOVED_DOSE from our casts' handlers | yes | yes | yes |
| SPELL_AURA_REMOVED by expiry (buffs, our target debuffs) | yes, at the modelled end | yes | yes |
| SPELL_PERIODIC_* ticks of our DoTs, HoTs and periodic energize | yes, on the tick grid (D4) | yes: tick *timing* is not probabilistic; amounts are not modelled (no consumer) | yes |
| SPELL_ENERGIZE from our casts | yes | yes | yes |
| SWING_DAMAGE (each hand's swing) | yes, on the swing clock | yes, as "a swing happens". Its rage uses the belief's **calibrated** per-swing rate, an observed average that already folds in the observed miss rate, not a proc. *Decision for the controller:* treat the base swing income as deterministic at every horizon. Alternative, if the user wants no outcome assumptions near: the landed rate inside the horizon and `L` × the landed rate beyond it (`L` = landed share, Comp §2.5). The crit term `(m − 1)c` is dropped at every horizon (crits are never assumed) | as inside, plus proc income (Unbridled Wrath) on the curve |
| SWING_MISSED / SPELL_MISSED / DODGE / PARRY | **never** as events | no | no. Their effect lives only in the calibrated rate. Overpower's availability after a dodge is a discrete proc and waits for the discrete rule |
| SPELL_ENERGIZE / SPELL_AURA_APPLIED from procs (Unbridled Wrath, Flurry, Clearcasting, Windfury's SPELL_EXTRA_ATTACKS) | additive ones yes, as expected value; discrete ones not yet | **no** (effectiveness 0) | yes, effectiveness × chance × value; discrete at the median arrival once that rule ships |
| `critical` flag on anything | **never** | no | no (user rule) |
| UNIT_DIED (target), PARTY_KILL, Victory Rush | never predicted (no time to die: health secret) | no | no (a user time-to-die setting could add it later) |
| SPELL_SUMMON / UNIT_DESTROYED / UNIT_DISSIPATES (our totems and guardians) | yes, at the modelled lifetime | yes | yes |
| SPELL_INTERRUPT (ours) | no event; only the interrupt's cooldown | — | — |
| ENCHANT_REMOVED (expiry) | yes, from the observed remaining time | yes | yes |
| Incoming damage, heals, CC on us | no (the observed LoC remaining time gates abilities) | — | — |

The bus should mark each forecast event with the horizon class it was emitted in, so a subscriber can ignore beyond-horizon proc events if it wants only deterministic ones.

Session O does not change the forecast rules: crits and misses are still never *assumed*. They are now *observed* as events (provenance observed, from `UNIT_COMBAT`) and can calibrate the belief's per-swing rate, the landed share `L` and the crit rate without any proxy.
