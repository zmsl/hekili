## 2. Resources on Forever

### 2.1 Energy (Feral)
- Max 100 (Classic default; no Druid max modifier found in the DB2; rogue Vigor is rogue-only) [DB2].
- **Regen is continuous, 10/s, not 20 per 2 s tick.** Basis: Blizzard 2026-09-30 class highlights as relayed in
  `docs/research/tdf/2026-10-13-forever-rogue-rotations.md:17` and `research-findings-for-lanes.md:50`; `GetPowerRegenForPowerType`
  energy read **10.001 plain out of combat, secret in combat** [P] (`probe-session-W-results.md:390`). Classic's Druid tick model
  (`value = 20` per `energy.tick_time_avg`, `Classic/Druid.lua:163-185`) must be dropped; copy Wrath's bare
  `spec:RegisterResource( Enum.PowerType.Energy )` and let VirtualState calibrate (`d.regen` default, EMA between ladder pins,
  `FW/VirtualState/Families/Resources.lua:123-134`).
- Secrecy: `UnitPower(player, Energy)` secret always; `C_Secrets.GetPowerTypeSecrecy(Energy)` = 2 [P]. Affordability is plain:
  `C_Spell.IsSpellUsable` + `insufficientPower` (gate spike). That is the **usability ladder**: each energy ability is a rung.
- **Ladder spenders (cost, all ranks identical in the DB2):** Rip 30 < Ferocious Bite 35 < Rake 40 < Claw 45 < Pounce 50 < Shred 60 =
  Ravage 60; Skull Bash 25 (rune-era), Cower 20, Swipe (Cat) 50 (rune-era 411128). Ferocity/Shredding Attacks lower Claw, Rake, Shred
  (section 5), so the ladder rungs move with talents (Warrior plan Q5: compare Snapshot `cost=` with `read=`).
- Shifting Power (1322605, Forever's Tiger's Fury replacement): ENERGIZE 40 energy, cost 55% base mana, cooldown 16 s, GCD 1 s,
  Cat Form only [DB2 effects: effect 30 base 40, SpellPower PowerCostPct 55; description "Instantly convert $c Mana into $s2 Energy";
  user memory `forever-feral-rotation.md`]. Improved Shifting Power 1322670: cooldown -4 s per rank (aura 107 misc 11; the research
  says -4/-8 s over 2 ranks) [DB2, G]. Tier 1 Feral 5-piece 1301247: -1 s [DB2].
- Feral Dedication 1231380/1231381/1231382: staying in Cat Form for a while (trigger period 5000 ms) raises energy regen +25%
  (aura 110 base 25) and Ferocious Bite damage; effect 1231382 base 50 [DB2]. Nobody has measured the delay or whether it is
  a talent; it would change `regen` (open Q3).
- Furor redesigned (17056, now Feral per TDF `TALENT_MOVES`): shifting into Cat restores 20% per rank of the energy you had on
  leaving Cat plus 10 energy per second spent out of it (1238181 base 40 dummy) [DB2 description, G]; **powershifting is gone**.
- Clearcasting (16870, 15 s, aura 108 -100: next damage or healing ability free) via Omen of Clarity (16864, a passive trained at 20
  on Forever) [DB2; research §1]. Not consumed by Wrath or zero-cost abilities [G].
- Energy-ability GCD is **1.0 s** for every cat ability (`StartRecoveryTime` 1000) [DB2]; Hekili's `gcd = "totem"` is the fixed 1 s
  GCD (`HK/State.lua:3190`), exactly what Classic's cat abilities already use. Classic's `RegisterOptions` has `gcd = 1126`
  (Mark of the Wild, 1.5 s); Task 12 of the facade plan changes the GCD spell to 61304.

- Open: combo points after a **missed finisher** (gate spike left open), after target swap and after the target dies (Q10).

### 2.3 Rage (Bear)
- Max 100 plain [P]; current secret always (`UnitPower` S 105/105 in combat and S 570/570 out, Warrior, `probe-session-W`).
- **The Warrior's speed formula** (`Forever/Warrior.lua` `rageOf`, lines ~85-98): rage per white swing = unhasted weapon speed x a rate
  (3.46 main hand one-hander, 4.5 two-hander, 1.73 off hand), independent of damage, level and haste (measured on Forever, sources
  magey/forever-warrior#3 and warrior compendium v3.1.0; level-22 checkpoint 2026-10-15: damage-based formula was off by half).
  Misses/dodges/parries give none, crits one extra base swing (ignored: `shortcut:`). The two-hander flag is Hekili's
  `main_hand.size` (gear scan). **Nothing says a bear follows it.** Classic's bear used the vanilla damage formula
  (`rage_amount()`, `Classic/Druid.lua:24-30`). If the speed rule holds in bear (swing 2.5 s, weapon-DPS auto-attack, research §1
  [G]): 2.5 x 3.46 = 8.65 per swing (one-hander) or 2.5 x 4.5 = 11.25 (two-hander, a staff/polearm) [I, untested]. Q8.
- **Decay.** Warrior: 1.25 rage/s out of combat (client tooltip; measured 100->0 in 82.2 s) and
  `GetPowerRegenForPowerType(rage)` = -12.499 in internal units, plain out of combat only [P]. The Warrior file declares it as the
  rage meta `decay = function () return 1.25 * ( 1 - 0.3 * Anger Management rank ) end`; TDF's Bear pack assumes the same
  (`TDF/src/TacoDogForever_Druid/Bear.lua`, `ns.BEAR_RAGE_DECAY`). Whether a bear decays at the same rate is unknown (Q9).
- **Max.** Gnome's `Expansive Mind` +5% does not apply: no Gnome Druid (section 8).
- **DB2 bear rage sources** (rage = DB2/10):
  - Enrage 5229: 10 rage now (effect 30 base 100) + 2/s for 10 s (aura 24 periodic energize base 20, 1000 ms) = 20, armor -27% Bear / -16%
    Dire, cooldown 60 s, off GCD. Aura 25503 (the 10 s buff, -26 armor effect) is the Classic `enrage` id 5229 vs 25503 split [DB2].
    The Warrior's Bloodrage is the template: `RegisterResource` rage entry `{ aura = "enrage", last = ..., interval = 1, value = 2 }`
    (Classic Druid already has exactly this entry, `Classic/Druid.lua:128-139`) plus the handler's instant `gain( 10, "rage" )`.
  - Furor 17056/17057: chance (20% per rank, research §1) of 10 rage (17057 effect 30 base 100) on entering Bear or Dire Bear [DB2, G].
    Classic's `swap_form` gives it only at rank 5 (`Classic/Druid.lua:89-98`): wrong on Forever.
  - Blood Frenzy 16958/16959: chance of 5 rage (energize 50) on a bear crit [DB2]; Natural Reaction 417051/417053: 5 rage on a dodge
    (chance in research: 20% per rank) [DB2, G]; Primal Fury merged into Blood Frenzy [G]. All unobservable events: modelled only
    through the VirtualState calibration scale.
  - Rage taken from damage: Stamina no longer reduces it (10-08), crits +100% [G]; damage taken is not readable, so it is not modelled.
- Rage costs (DB2 /10): Maul 15 (Ferocity -1/rank), Swipe 20 (-1), Primal Bite 20 (-1), Lacerate 15 (Shredding Attacks), Bash 10,
  Demoralizing Roar 10, Challenging Roar 15, Feral Charge 5; Frenzied Regeneration 0 (converts up to 10 rage/s), Growl 0, Enrage 0.
- **Maul is a next-swing strike** like Heroic Strike: `onNextSwing = true` (Warrior Cleave, `Forever/Warrior.lua` cleave entry),
  `usable = not buff.maul.up`, queued state from `C_Spell.IsCurrentSpell( 6807 )` in `reset_precast` (Warrior does this for Heroic
  Strike). Classic's `maul` aura duration is `swings.mainhand_speed` and a `QueueAuraExpiration` finish hook; the Warrior plan
  replaced that model with VirtualState's `onNextSwing`. Cost has no GCD and no cooldown row [DB2: Maul has no SpellCooldowns row].
- **Clearcasting spend rule (TDF)**: Classic Hekili wrote `(buff.clearcasting.up and 0) or cost` in each `spend`. TDF's
  `energyCost`/`ferocityCost` helpers do the same (Feral.lua, "An energy cost that Clearcasting waives") and `reads = { CLEARCASTING }`.
  The facade's `RegisterCostMod{ aura = "clearcasting", pct = -1, charges = 1, spells = {...} }` is the declarative form
  (`FW/docs/.../specs/2026-10-15-virtualstate-event-facade.md` §12b: "Clearcasting is masked the same way ... excludes Wrath, Thrash
  and Savage Roar", "`pct = -1, charges = 1` is Clearcasting's shape"). That needs an Omen of Clarity per-class spell list, since the DB2
  effect is family-masked; get the masks with the wowdb query the Gnome file's comment gives (SpellClassOptions) -- not done here.

## 6. Forms and the shapeshift gates

**What the client exposes.** `GetShapeshiftForm()` and `GetShapeshiftFormInfo(i)` (fourth return = form spell ID, which is how the
Warrior maps stance to aura) are plain [`HK/docs/forever-facade-notes.md:115-116`; Warrior probe PR10]. **Caveat that the Warrior
could not settle:** the Warrior's stance read plain in combat (`GetShapeshiftFormID` 17, 200/200), "but Druid forms are auras (Catnip: no
Druid aura readable in combat) and a stance is not a form aura. Session D must record `ff`, `powerType` and `bonusOffset` while
shifting in combat" (`probe-session-W-results.md:611`). So the plain read is **unverified for Druid** (Q2). TDF's design assumption is the
opposite: "in combat Forever hides the form's aura, so leaving the form mid-fight isn't seen" (`Feral.lua` comment) and it infers the
form from usability (`form_from_usability` tracker: Shred/Claw/Rake "not usable" for a non-energy reason means out of Cat Form).

**How the Warrior did it (to copy).** `STANCE_OF_SPELL = { [2457]="battle_stance", ... }` and, in `reset_precast`: remove the three
stance auras, `GetShapeshiftForm()` -> `GetShapeshiftFormInfo( form )` 4th return -> `applyBuff( stance )` (so the bar order never matters,
ruling W5); stance abilities have `nobuff = <their own>`, `gcd = "off"`, a shared `timeToReady` (category cooldown), a
`swap_stance` StateFunction (removes the other auras, `spend( lost, "rage" )` above what a swap keeps, applies the new stance); other
abilities gate with `buff = "berserker_stance"` or, where several stances allow it, a `buff` function returning the current stance's aura
(`shield_bash`, Charge with Vanguard). Hekili's own `stance` table is NOT used (it reads a fifth return that does not exist, State.lua 2617-2631).
Warrior checkpoint Q1 asked the user per stance which abilities work/refuse; the Druid needs the same (Q2).

**Druid port.** Classic's `swap_form( form )` (`Classic/Druid.lua:83-112`) removes `form` and `maul`, then: bear form -> spend all rage, +10
if Furor 5/5, +5 with Wolfshead; cat form -> +40 energy (Furor 5/5), +20 (Wolfshead). On Forever: bear -> rage lost to 0 (`swap_form` spend
current) then Furor chance (not a certainty: the handler cannot know; take 0 or the expectation 2*rank), cat -> Furor restores 20%/rank of
the energy held on leaving Cat plus regen out of form (stateful; do not model beyond "energy kept"), mana cost 55% x (1 - 0.1 x NS rank).
`cat_form`, `bear_form`, `dire_bear_form`, `moonkin_form`, `travel_form`, `aquatic_form` are all instant, 1.5 s GCD, no cooldown.
Add a StateFunction `swap_form` and a `form` alias aura, `reset_precast` mapping the form's spell ID (768, 5487, 9634, 24858, 783, 1066) to
its aura, and `buff = "cat_form"`/`"bear_form"` gates. Dire Bear is bear's rank 2 (TDF `ns.RegisterForms`, copy of Bear).

**DB2 form hints (no SpellShapeshift table).**
- Forms are `SPELL_EFFECT` 6 + aura 36 (MOD_SHAPESHIFT) with `misc0` = form id: Cat 1, Bear 5, Dire Bear 8, Travel 3, Aquatic 4,
  Moonkin 31 [DB2 effects 768, 5487, 9634, 783, 1066, 24858].
- The newer Forever spells carry the form in their description template: `$?a5487|a9634|a3025[...Requires Cat Form, Bear Form, Dire Bear Form]`
  (Berserk 417141), `Requires Bear Form, Dire Bear Form` (Feral Charge 1238122), `Requires Cat Form` (Tiger's Fury 5217). The aura IDs in
  such tests are the forms' passives (3025 cat, 1178/21178 bear, 9635 dire). Classic-era spells (Shred, Maul, ...) carry no such text.
- Attribute `SpellMisc.Attributes_2 & 0x80000` (NOT_NEED_SHAPESHIFT) is set for Wrath, Starfire, Moonfire, Healing Touch, Rejuvenation,
  Mark of the Wild, Innervate; not set for Claw, Shred, Rake, Rip, Ferocious Bite, Ravage, Pounce, Maul, Swipe, Bash, Demoralizing Roar,
  Enrage, Frenzied Regeneration, Growl, Cower, Prowl, Primal Bite, Lacerate, Shifting Power(256 only), Berserk. Cast-from-form is therefore
  not a Forever change for them. Which form each non-flagged spell wants is Classic knowledge: cat abilities need Cat (and Ravage/Pounce
  Prowl), Maul/Swipe/Bash/Demoralizing Roar/Enrage/Frenzied Regeneration/Growl/Challenging Roar need Bear or Dire Bear (Feral Charge
